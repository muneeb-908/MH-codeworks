OVERVIEW

About The Project

Lost Link is a web-based application designed to connect people who have lost items with those who have found them. The platform allows users to report lost or found items, upload supporting proof, communicate securely and claim ownership through a structured verification process.

The system also includes an administrative dashboard for managing users, reports, rewards, categories and verification requests while maintaining a responsive experience across all devices.

KEY FEATURES

What Lost Link Offers

Lost Link simplifies the lost and found process through AI-powered matching, secure verification and an intuitive platform for users and administrators.

AI Smart Matching

AI analyzes lost and found reports to identify potential matches quickly and improve recovery chances.

Lost & Found Reports

Users can easily submit, browse and manage lost or found item reports.

Proof Verification

Ownership verification helps ensure that recovered items are returned to the rightful owners.

Admin Dashboard

A centralized dashboard for managing reports, users, categories and platform activities.

Reward System

Users earn points and badges for successfully helping recover lost items.

Responsive Experience

Fully responsive interface designed for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

The Challenge

Many lost items never reach their owners because there is no centralized platform that allows secure reporting, verification and communication between users.

Our Solution

Lost Link provides a centralized platform where users can report lost or found items, upload proofs, earn rewards and interact through a secure dashboard managed by administrators.
